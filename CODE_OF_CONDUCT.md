# Soundwave community standards

We aim to make participation in Soundwave respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Offer constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, or discriminate against others.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or personal data without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, intimidation, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

This source copy does not yet have a Soundwave-owned public reporting channel. Before opening any project space, its maintainers should publish a private, project-controlled contact route. Do not send sensitive reports to the upstream Echo-Music project or include private data in public issues.

This code applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing Soundwave.
