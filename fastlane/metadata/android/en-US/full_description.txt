Soundwave is a local-first Android music player. You can browse without connecting an account; optional third-party sign-ins and sync features can be enabled in Settings.

Features:
* Search and play music from supported providers.
* Background playback with lock-screen and media controls.
* Synchronized lyrics with multiple display styles.
* Local playlists, queue management, favorites, and library tools.
* Offline downloads for supported content.
* Music recognition, equalizer, and audio-output controls.
* Listening history stored on-device. Android automatic backup and device transfer exclude the history database.

Cloud AI prompt features and AI lyric translation are not included. Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies.
